### 论文与评测

- HNSW：Malkov & Yashunin，*Efficient and robust approximate nearest neighbor search using Hierarchical Navigable Small World graphs* — <https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.09320>
- DPR：Karpukhin 等，*Dense Passage Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering*（EMNLP 2020）— <https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.04906>
- Sentence-BERT：Reimers & Gurevych，*Sentence-BERT: Sentence Embeddings using Siamese BERT-Networks*（EMNLP 2019）— <https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.10084>
- SPLADE：Formal、Piwowarski、Clinchant，*SPLADE: Sparse Lexical and Expansion Model for First Stage Ranking*（SIGIR 2021 short）— <https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.05720>
- ColBERT：Khattab & Zaharia，*ColBERT: Efficient and Effective Passage Search via Contextualized Late Interaction over BERT*（SIGIR 2020）— <https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12832>
- HyDE：Gao、Ma、Lin、Callan，*Precise Zero-Shot Dense Retrieval without Relevance Labels* — <https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10496>
- BEIR：Thakur 等，*BEIR: A Heterogenous Benchmark for Zero-shot Evaluation of Information Retrieval Models*（NeurIPS 2021 Datasets and Benchmarks）— <https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.08663>
- MTEB：Muennighoff 等，*MTEB: Massive Text Embedding Benchmark* — <https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.07316>
- *ColBERTv2: Effective and Efficient Retrieval via Lightweight Late Interaction*（NAACL 2022）— <https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.01488>
- *Document Ranking with a Pretrained Sequence-to-Sequence Model*（Findings of EMNLP 2020）— <https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.06713>
- *Is ChatGPT Good at Search? Investigating Large Language Models as Re-Ranking Agents*（EMNLP 2023）— <https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.09542>
- *Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts*（TACL 2023）— <https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172>
- *Found in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts Better via Plug-and-Play Positional Encoding*（ACL Findings 2024）— <https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16008>
- *Retrieval Augmented Generation or Long-Context LLMs? A Comprehensive Study and Hybrid Approach*（EMNLP 2024 Industry Track）— <https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16833>
- *Self-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection* — <https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11511>
- *LLMLingua: Compressing Prompts for Accelerated Inference of Large Language Models*（EMNLP 2023）— <https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05736>
- *RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation* — <https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15217>
- *Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena*（NeurIPS 2023 Datasets and Benchmarks Track）— <https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685>

### 工程文档与源码

- Anthropic，*Introducing Contextual Retrieval*（Contextual Embeddings / Contextual BM25、失败率 5.7%→3.7%→2.9%→1.9%、每百万文档 token 约 1.02 美元、50–100 token 上下文前置、块数 5/10/20 的对比）— <https://www.anthropic.com/research/contextual-retrieval>
- Elasticsearch 文档，*Reciprocal rank fusion*（RRF 公式、`rank_constant` 默认 60、`rank_window_size` 语义与示例、指向 Cormack SIGIR 2009 论文 PDF）— <https://www.elastic.co/docs/reference/elasticsearch/rest-apis/reciprocal-rank-fusion>
- Elasticsearch 文档，*Similarity settings*（BM25 为默认相似度、`k1` 默认 1.2、`b` 默认 0.75、`discount_overlaps`；LM Dirichlet `mu` 默认 2000；LM Jelinek Mercer `lambda` 默认 0.1）— <https://www.elastic.co/docs/reference/elasticsearch/index-settings/similarity>
- LangChain 源码，`libs/text-splitters/langchain_text_splitters/character.py`（默认分隔符 `["\n\n", "\n", " ", ""]`，各语言分隔符表，Markdown 按 ATX 标题 `\n#{1,6} ` 切分）— <https://github.com/langchain-ai/langchain/blob/master/libs/text-splitters/langchain_text_splitters/character.py>
